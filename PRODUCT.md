# Frontdrop

A free native macOS wallpaper app for this MacBook. Success means choosing an animated scene and seeing it behind desktop icons, without a subscription or account.

Confirmed brief: investigate installed Backdrop, preserve inspectable evidence, and build a similar app for free. Reference: Cindori Backdrop. Original source and bundled artwork only; user-owned videos can be imported. No dependency on Backdrop installation, licence, or community service.

Implementation assumptions: SwiftUI/AppKit for native controls; Metal for original ambient scenes and AVFoundation for videos. Desktop playback is the first delivery. Lock Screen parity is unproven and excluded from this delivery. Confirmed scene preferences: abstract, nature, and anime/game-inspired (all three).

Updated preference (19–20 October 2026): prioritise real animated anime/game sequences and moving environments/camera journeys over subtly animated still-character artwork. Both types matter. The user rejected the animation archive/stock-footage additions. Discovery now prioritises full wallpaper libraries, with honest motion previews and separate community references. The user owns Wallpaper Engine; downloaded Video projects can be imported, while its interactive projects remain unsupported.
