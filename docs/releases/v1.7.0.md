## A smoother, clearer Frontdrop

- A redesigned dark gallery opens directly to your library, with larger wallpaper cards, gentle hover feedback, and clear Previewing and On desktop states.
- Buttons respond to hover and presses. Motion respects the macOS Reduce Motion preference, and controls keep keyboard access and descriptive labels.
- Display and video framing controls have visible labels and explanations. Empty collections offer a useful next step.
- Your active wallpaper and Pause / Resume / Stop controls stay visible while browsing online sources.
- A simpler source directory and a reorganised browser toolbar make websites, collections, navigation and downloads easier to understand.
- Settings now has separate Playback, Library and Updates tabs. Use **Settings → Updates → Check for Updates** to find future downloads.

Requires macOS 14 or later. The DMG is for Apple silicon Macs and includes a SHA-256 checksum. Quit Frontdrop before replacing your installed app. This community build is ad-hoc signed and is not notarized by Apple.
